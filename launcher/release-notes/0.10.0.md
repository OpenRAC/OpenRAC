## Highlights

- **Online co-op for Ratchet & Clank.** With the Multiplayer mod, players share one campaign: each game shows the other players' Ratchet with his wrench and weapon, crates broken and bolts collected in one game count in the others, and the party travels between planets together (#99).
- **The level editor tells you where you are.** The Level editor tab is one status panel with the next step, a progress bar, your levels, plain error messages, and an Update project that carries your edits over (#107). Start over reads the levels again or resets the editor (#105).
- **An Exit item** in the main menu and the pause menu, with a Yes/No question in the game's five languages (#110).
- **The Drone Device makes drones again**, and Novalis's sparks no longer pile up at Ratchet's feet as a white orb (#110).
- **The camera follows the ship** as it leaves a planet (#96), and **movies play in the game's language** (#98).
- **Native mods on Android** (new) (#110), and the launcher now runs on **Android 9** (#77).

## Gameplay and fixes

### Ratchet & Clank (PAL and US discs)

- The Drone Device spent ammo and made no drones, and Novalis's sparks gathered into a white orb: some of the game's code read values it never set, and the port's compiler dropped that code. Such values now start at zero, as the console's memory has them (#110).
- The camera no longer stands still in the wrong place when the ship flies off from a planet (#96).
- Infobots and story movies play in the language the game is set to, not always in English (#98).
- Ratchet keeps his O2 mask in Gemlik's ship scenes (#65).
- Exit in Giant Clank's Quit menu makes Clank small again (#81).
- The camera's collision object is made again, as on the console, for the camera tricks that use it (camera proxy and flip) (#80).
- Umbris's water puzzle no longer shows flat lilac slabs over the water (#69).
- Kerwan's air traffic keeps flying at the sides of a wide picture instead of stopping dead. It is a new game fix, which can be switched back to the original.
- **More room for effects** (767 object slots) is now a game fix you can switch off (#110).
- After a save is loaded during play, the old level's last picture no longer shows behind the loading picture or the story card.
- The parked ship's thruster glow on Pokitaru no longer shows through the ship when seen from behind.
- The zapper bots' ground glow on Oltanis draws with its own texture, not as a lit square on the floor.
- Smoke and fire spawners run the game's own code; they were left out before.
- The game keeps its speed when drawing falls behind, and the classic pond ripples behave as on the console again.
- The US disc: Ratchet's held item is posed correctly.
- If the game's camera is ever unusable, the last good view is kept instead of drawing the world from it, and the log says so. It is a guard for a black screen with long coloured lines that a player reported around a ship trip (#114).
- The Video page and its new Scenery detail setting are in the PAL disc's five languages (#67).
- More of the game runs the decompilation's exact C: rac1-decomp and the NTSC decomp are synced (#106), and the US version now runs PAL's matched C for 14 more functions.

### Going Commando

- With Developer tools on, Going Commando's Play builds the game from your disc image and starts it, behind a warning: it reaches the main menu and stops at the first level load. It is not playable yet.

## Online co-op

The Multiplayer mod itself is released separately and installed from the Mods tab. The port now gives it what it needs:

- Other players appear as figures that look like Ratchet, hold the other player's wrench and weapon, and fire their shots (#99); their gloves open and throw with the hand (#120).
- One game runs the level and the others show it: crates broken in one game break in the others, and the bolts the host's game gives belong to the player who collected them.
- Opening the pause menu no longer stops or empties the party's world: the level keeps running live behind the menu (#99).
- A party's cutscenes, landings, take-offs and flights end together, and Start skips the movies after a co-op start (#99).
- The party travels between planets together, and a player whose host leaves goes back to the title.
- **A Multiplayer name** in Settings, General: 3 to 16 letters, digits and underscores, made up for you at the first start. Every online mod shows the same name, and a mod's settings can default to it.
- Mods can add items to the title's main menu and the pause menu's Options page, with pages built from the game's own menus, widened to fit their text.

## Level editor and modding

- **One status panel** for the Level editor tab: it finds out by itself what changed (scenes saved after the last pack, outdated projects, packs for another disc version, missing files) and offers the one next step: Read the levels and open, Open in Godot, Pack your changes, Play with your edits, Update project or Repair (#107).
- **Update project keeps your work**: it backs the old project up, reads fresh levels and replays only your changes to mobys. What it can't carry over is listed, with a button to open the backup (#107).
- Each step shows its progress as a bar, Your levels stays open under the status, and errors are plain sentences with a next step; the tool's raw output is behind Show details (#107).
- **Start over** under Troubleshoot: read a disc's levels again, or reset the level editor, with an option to also remove packed levels and imported edits. Each one lists what it deletes and what it keeps, and never touches your disc data or saves (#105).
- **Edits on the PAL disc reach the game.** The game plays the PAL disc at 60 Hz and loads that copy of each level's gameplay data, but the editor packed the 50 Hz copy: crate edits were reported as packed while the game loaded the untouched data. Older PAL projects are moved over (#87).
- Typed pvar edits work with the installed launcher's tools, not only from a source checkout (#100).
- **The level's background colour** from LevelSettings is used in the game. It is the colour behind the sky, so it shows where the sky does not cover it, such as on Rilgar and Umbris (#108).
- **HD sky textures**: the sky's stars and glows take texture overrides as the sky shells do, and the sky model can be exported as `sky.gltf` (#108).
- **Map packs**: an edited level can be exported as a standalone map mod and played as a session of its own, with its own name and start point (#84).
- **Ship destinations** (PAL only, early work): add your own worlds to the ship's destinations instead of replacing campaign planets, and import static scenery from another level with its textures and collision (#104).
- The Level editor tab summarises your prepared level edits and asset packs (#91).
- The installed launcher's Godot project gets the Collision dock again; its `plugin.cfg` was left out.

## Launcher

- **Development channel**: it now follows the `develop` branch, so Development builds get the newest work again (#111).
- Steam Deck: opening a page no longer crashes the launcher where the webview runs without compositing (#63).
- Linux with Wayland and NVIDIA (hybrid GPUs): the first click no longer crashes the launcher (#68). Not yet tested on NVIDIA hardware.
- A Linux tablet or handheld is no longer taken for Android, so choosing the games folder or a disc image works there.
- Play no longer asks to check the levels again and again after they were read; the button says Check and play.
- A Mods checkout whose build is older than its sources is refused with a message to build the mods again; its script mods are taken from source.
- The actions menu stays open when it opens on Android-sized windows (#88, #89).

## Graphics

- **No more thin lines along texture seams.** Many textures the game clamps at their edges were drawn repeating, which pulled a line of another colour along terrain, scenery and the sky. Your levels are read from the disc again for this version.
- **Scenery detail: Original** (Video page): keeps to the game's own draw distances for scenery, for weaker devices. Full stays the default.
- Faster frames on weaker devices: the mobys' poses, the HUD's text and the game's memory lookups take less time.
- After an update, Vulkan never reuses pipelines from another build.
- **Record diagnostics** (Ctrl+F3, the Developer menu, or the Android drawer): a short recording of timings, system and graphics details, saved and copied as a report to paste into an issue.
- Enhanced graphics (experimental):
  - Early lighting for interiors (many are still too dark), Novalis's shallow water with wakes, Ratchet getting wet, rim lighting on important objects, and glowing Novalis mushrooms and Blarg Station lamps.
  - A settings window of its own, a sunlight angle you can change, and High or Medium shadow quality.
  - Enhanced graphics now needs the level's baked lighting, and its effects turn off without it.
  - Ambient occlusion costs less (#83); FSR upscaling and sharpening are in the in-game drawer; late effects stay hidden behind the world with FSR.

## Mod SDK

- SDK 1.5.0, 1.7.0, 1.10.0 and 1.10.1 are released, with interfaces 1.4 to 1.10:
  - 1.4: the graphics context, views of the game and a shared Vulkan device, for a VR mod.
  - 1.5: an entry in the title's Options with a page the mod draws.
  - 1.6: tick-boundary and snapshot calls (savestates).
  - 1.7: pages built from the game's own menus, and items in the main menu.
  - 1.8: play calls for multiplayer: status, the hero's pose, figures, starting a game and travelling, saves.
  - 1.9: the world: the level's mobys, driving and posing them, hits, scenes, held items and shots, pause-menu items, notices, and the world kept running under menus.
  - 1.10: desktop menus of a mod's own, for persistent savestates that survive a restart (#103).
- The launcher knows interface 1.10, so it offers mods built for it.
- New: an `android-arm64` platform for native mods, built by the template's workflow with the NDK (#110), in SDK 1.10.1 (#117, #118). A mod shows as not built for Android until it is released with 1.10.1 or later; the Ratchet & Clank mods already are.

## For developers

- **Branch model**: work lands on `develop` through squash-merged pull requests; `master` is protected and moves only by a release pull request. The history was squashed on 2026-10-28 (the old one is on `backup/master-pre-squash`), and RELEASING.md describes the steps (#111).
- hostgen starts locals the decompiled C leaves unset at zero, which cleared all 44 NTSC and 13 PAL uninitialised-use warnings (#110).
- rac1 synced to rac1-decomp 0f0bfcfe (#106) and the NTSC decomp 8a5972b3; NTSC duplicate functions resolved (#109); `carry_functions.py` reads the source's overrides, readable names and `--replace`; rows hostgen no longer reads are gone.
- A backfill gate for rac2's boot and level 00 code with the retail interpreter, kept as a test-only tree (#85); rac2's functional C table is read as candidates.
- Optional Tracy profiling, a simulated slower processor and a sampler of the hottest functions in diagnostics recordings; recordings also carry effects, particles, free moby slots and collision queries near Ratchet (#110).
- Developer-only launcher actions with a warning to accept first (`"developer"` and `"warning"` in `actions.json`).
- CI: a slow package mirror and one failed system no longer hold the build up; faster launcher Rust builds (rust-lld on Windows); action updates (#70); formatting (#112).
- Tools: the rac1 overlay triage (#72), three backported PAL functions (#73), Windows export and bake retries, and the docs follow the NTSC decomp's new layout.

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS. Your levels are read from the disc again for this version.
