## Highlights

- **Going Commando is playable, early and unstable.** It runs as a native port on PC (US disc), and Play and the enhanced lighting bake no longer need Developer tools. Expect wrong graphics, missing effects and crashes (#206, #212).
- **Vulkan is now the default renderer** and is no longer marked experimental (#188).
- **HDR output** on Vulkan, for HDR10 and scRGB screens (#204).
- **An Audio tab in the launcher** replaces the game's sounds, music and speech (#164).
- **Mod index**: `openrac://` links, dependencies and auto-update for mods (#161).
- **One in-game menu** on desktop and Android, in the launcher's look (#211).
- **Mod SDK 1.12.0**: mods can drive the hero, add props and sounds, and move the camera (#203).

## Gameplay and fixes

### Ratchet & Clank

- Drowning on Quartu without the O2 mask no longer crashes (#152).
- The PAL disc no longer freezes after picking Quartu (#151).
- A landing with a full moby pool skips its missing actors instead of crashing (#147).
- The planet's name shows in the flight between planets, and the stop after it is shorter (#156).
- With the HUD at the edges, the weapon background spans its panel (#207) and the Suck Cannon gauge is no longer empty (#150).
- The console's 224 moby class slots are kept (#158).

### Going Commando

- Joba's taxi rides end (#265).
- Pause Options has the Video item, the ship HUD sits at the edges, and glyphs have no lines on them (#239).
- Feltzin's ship input matches the original game (#241).
- Windows x64 crashes are fixed, including one on Endako (#228, #232).
- The launcher offers a rebuild when the game's build is older than its sources (#212).

## Graphics

- **Borderless fullscreen** replaces exclusive fullscreen (#258).
- **A field of view slider** for gameplay (#249).
- Distant mobys and shrubs fade as they do on the PS2 (#210).
- Shaders build behind the loading screen on Vulkan and OpenGL (#216).
- Vulkan: faster water and draw uploads (#255), white moby glow colours are kept (#208), and Mali GPUs no longer crash at the title screen (#263).
- No more sound gaps on Windows (#188).
- Enhanced graphics:
  - Native baking, compressed probes and water materials (#154), and one place for material overrides (#246).
  - A configurable FFT ocean and optional fur shells (#254), and jittered atmosphere shadow samples (#253).
  - Playable Clank casts shadow volumes (#217).
  - The NTSC level 1 and level 11 probe bakes finish (#152), and probe paths check for clearance in both directions (#189).

## Launcher

- Mod updates are offered at start, with saved mod links and a mods mark on saves (#123).
- Copying and installing a mod file shows its progress (#209).
- **Safe rendering** turns on after a crashed start (#131).
- **Add the launcher to Steam**, with its pictures (#184).
- The installer has a new look, the overlay scales at 4K, and the first start is fullscreen (#177).
- The launcher wears the picked game's accent colour on desktop, and switching games no longer slides the page (#179, #185, #231).
- Controller: smooth navigation, one press of A acts, and a game's tile is one control (#235, #240).
- The save editor names Going Commando's arena challenges (#244).
- WebKit compositing is off on Wayland with NVIDIA (#130).
- A development build can switch back to an older Stable release (#139).

## Controls

- A diagonal on the stick moves as fast as a straight push (#168).
- Gamepads work behind a USB-C dock, and you can switch controllers (#149).
- Controls and camera respond at any frame rate, with a smoother frame limiter (#142, #146).
- The Steam Controller works without Steam (#177).

## Android

- Faster Enhanced graphics on phone GPUs (#165).
- The drawer has motion blur, colour correction, texture upscaling and a reset (#183).
- The performance hint keeps the CPU clocks from running late (#181).
- Vulkan keeps the world in one render pass, which stops frame rate drops on tiled phone GPUs (#267).
- Low-latency frames run VSync at the display's rate (#142).
- Going Commando's flights export on Android (#230).

## Level editor and modding

- Expanded Ratchet & Clank level authoring and runtime limits for modded levels (#148).
- The editor reads levels below normal priority, leaving threads free (#144).

## Mod SDK

- SDK 1.11.0 and 1.12.0 are released, with interfaces 1.11 and 1.12:
  - 1.11: a data folder per mod, and the save slot in use (#172).
  - 1.12: the hero moved by a mod, the level's collision, up to 256 props, sounds, the camera and heat haze (#203).
- New Ratchet & Clank hook points for menus, the HUD, the vendor, ammo drops and saves (#136).

## For developers

- Debug tools: Object Viewer and Forward+ local lighting (#195), freecam and culling inspection (#196), and a freecam on the gamepad (#259).
- Bounded main and render crash breadcrumbs (#193).

The installers are built by the launcher workflow and are not signed for Windows or macOS.
